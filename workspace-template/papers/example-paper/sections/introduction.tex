\section{Introduction}

Retrieval-augmented generation has become central to question answering over the past decade.
Models now consult external documents before they answer.
This reduces hallucination but adds a retrieval step that can fail silently.

Despite recent advances in retrieval, long-context grounding remains largely unexplored.
Existing approaches either truncate the context or re-rank at every step, which limits their use on long documents.
